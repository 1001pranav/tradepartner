# M3: Risk engine, modes, paper broker, done

## What exists
- `risk/rules.py`: 38 one-function-per-rule checks returning `RuleResult` (`block` or `halt`), all pure. `risk/sizing.py` computes quantity with exact `Fraction` arithmetic (strategies never set quantity).
- `risk/engine.py`: `RiskEngine.check()` is a pure function of `(proposal, context)`, runs **all** rules and collects every failure. It is the only place an `ApprovedOrder` is minted. `check_halts()` is the 30-second tracker view; `enforce_halts()` applies halts to mode state; `approve_emergency_exit()` is the kill-switch path.
- `risk/approved.py` + `risk/_mint.py`: `ApprovedOrder` cannot be constructed without the private token; import-linter forbids every module except `risk.engine` and `risk.approved` from importing it.
- `risk/limits.py`, `risk/context.py`: loss trackers rebuilt from the ledger (they include manual trades), consecutive losses per strategy, stop dates for the cooldown, order counters.
- `modes/state.py` (read-only) and `modes/controller.py` (mutations): never starts in AUTO, system may only lower, AUTO needs per-strategy unlock (approved model, >= 40 closed trades, >= 2 months, gate passed, name typed), halts force OFF, reset needs `RESET` and lands in MANUAL. Every transition is written to `mode_state` and `audit_log`. Only `cli`, `ui` and `risk.engine` may import the controller (import-linter).
- `execution/`: `Broker` protocol, `PaperBroker` (simulated exchange that persists a real paper ledger and simulates GTT OCO), `Router` (the only caller of `place_order`), `KillSwitch` (cancel entries, square off bot MIS, 60 s fallback to protected market orders, leave CNC and GTT alone, notify).
- `runtime.py`: composition root. `notify.py`: macOS + optional Telegram (Keychain).
- CLI: `mode`, `unlock-auto`, `kill`. UI: Modes and Risk pages (limits are read-only; halt reset needs `RESET`; kill needs `KILL`).

## Acceptance
- 100% branch coverage on `risk`, `modes`, `costs`, `ledger`, `execution`.
- Property tests (hypothesis, 800 examples, with a guard proving approvals are actually reached): the engine never approves qty < 1, notional above the position cap, a missing stop, risk above 0.75%, a limit outside the price band, an entry outside the time window, or any entry past a loss/order limit. Exits never exceed the position.
- Simulated day: a 25% collapse in an AUTO position breaches the 1% AUTO daily loss, `enforce_halts` forces global OFF, new entries are rejected, a protective exit still runs.
- A restart (`ModeController.startup`) clamps AUTO to MANUAL and nothing is auto-placed afterwards.

## Spec questions (marked in code)
- Protective exits are exempt from the daily order count so a capped-out day cannot trap a position (the per-second limit still applies).
- Emergency exits are tagged `tpk-<hash>` (no proposal exists) and recognised by the ledger sync as bot actions.
- `order_proposals` gained `strategy`, `kind`, `exit_reason` (migration 0002); needed for exits, cooldowns and per-strategy streaks.
