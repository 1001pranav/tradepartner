# M9: Intraday, code complete; PAPER ONLY by construction

Intraday is built last and gated hardest (spec 1, 16-M9). Everything here runs on the paper broker until the evidence below exists; **the risk engine refuses a live MIS entry** until it does.

## What exists
- `data/live.py`: `BarBuilder` turns ticks into 5-minute bars (IST session buckets, per-symbol volume deltas, bar `ts` = its END time so as-of filtering is leak-proof). `TickerService` wraps `KiteTicker` for a WebSocket feed; until you wire that, the `poll_quotes` job feeds the same builder from polled quotes. No LLM and no network in the bar path.
- `features/intraday.py`: causal 5-minute features (within-day returns, VWAP distance, opening range and breakout, volume ratio, ATR, minutes since open). The opening range is NaN until its third bar has closed. Labels: next-30-minute return measured from the NEXT bar's open, same session only.
- `strategies/intraday.py`: opening-range / VWAP breakout setup, long only, MIS, entries 09:30-14:30 IST, stale bars rejected, news-blocked symbols excluded (precomputed in the morning), filtered by the model (`min_prob`). Geometry: stop within 1.5%, reward:risk >= 1.5.
- `models/intraday.py`: walk-forward LightGBM on 5-minute bars, evaluated by the same seven-check gate (bar-by-bar simulation, doubled-slippage test, 40% feature cap). Registers a **candidate** only.
- `signals/intraday.py`: one idea per symbol per day (append-only signals), shadow models logged only.
- `execution/mis_protect.py`: every bot MIS position gets an exchange SL-M order, placed **through the router** as a protective exit (so it passes the risk engine), and replaced if the position size changes. The 15:10 square-off from M7 closes whatever is left.
- Risk: `rule_intraday_gate`. With the real broker, a MIS entry is blocked unless there is an approved intraday model whose paper gate passed AND at least 40 closed paper trades over at least 3 months. Paper trading is always allowed. Swing/positional are unaffected.
- AUTO for intraday has its own, stricter unlock: **3 months** of tracking instead of 2 (same 40 trades, approved model and typed confirmation).
- Jobs: `intraday_scan` (every 5 min) and `mis_protect` (every 30 s). `config/strategies/intraday_v1.yaml` ships with `enabled: false`.
- CLI: `tradepartner data backfill --interval 5minute`, `tradepartner train --strategy intraday_v1`.

## Acceptance
- Gate and mechanics are tested on synthetic 5-minute data (causality of features, no cross-day labels, scanner only sees finished bars, signals, stops, the live-intraday block, the stricter unlock).
- **Pending, yours:** at least 3 months of paper trading must pass the gate before any MANUAL live intraday. Suggested path: backfill 5-minute history, `train --strategy intraday_v1`, review, `models shadow`, set `enabled: true`, run on the paper broker for 3 months, then `models approve`. AUTO intraday needs the separate unlock.

## Honest expectations
SEBI's FY23 study found about 7 in 10 individual intraday traders lost money. A strategy that cannot beat its baseline after costs in walk-forward is not deployed. Treat intraday as the experiment it is, with the smallest size you can accept.
