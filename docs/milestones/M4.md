# M4: Backtester and baselines, done

## What exists
- `backtest/engine.py`: event-driven daily backtester. Signals at close T become orders for the open of T+1. It runs the **same `RiskEngine`** as live (it only builds `Context` from simulated state), applies the full Zerodha cost model and adverse slippage, and is deterministic (ties broken by symbol, no randomness).
- `backtest/fills.py`: limit orders fill only if the bar touches the limit; stop and target inside one bar means the stop is hit first; gap-through-stop exits at the open; targets are resting limits (no slippage); locked-circuit bars do not fill.
- `backtest/view.py`: `MarketView` is point-in-time. `history(symbol, as_of)` can never return a bar dated after `as_of`.
- `backtest/metrics.py`: CAGR, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, Calmar, hit rate, average win/loss, turnover, cost drag, exposure, trade count; per-year and per-regime (Nifty vs 200-day average, using the previous day's regime).
- `backtest/benchmarks.py`: Nifty 50 buy-and-hold and equal-weight universe (monthly rebalance, with costs).
- `strategies/baseline.py`: 20-day momentum top-N (swing) and 12-1 month momentum top-N (positional), both through the risk engine.
- `backtest/report.py`: HTML report saved under `~/.tradepartner/reports/` with costs, slippage, rejection reasons, trade list and the survivorship banner when the universe is static.
- CLI: `tradepartner backtest [--strategy ...] [--capital-inr ...]`.

## Acceptance
- Deterministic regression: the same synthetic data produces an identical trade list across runs, pinned by a golden digest in `tests/unit/test_backtest_engine.py` (update it deliberately if behaviour changes).
- Baseline report renders; costs, slippage and risk-rejection reasons are in it.
- Lookahead test: scrambling all data after `as_of` never changes either baseline's candidates.

## Spec questions / modelling choices
- Circuit rule: spec says "open equals high"; taken literally it blocks ordinary gap-ups. We require a fully locked bar (open == high == low) moving away from the prior close.
- Time-stop exits go through the risk engine (as a protective EXIT); stop and target exits are treated as broker-side GTT orders and bypass it, as in live trading.
- Weekly-loss and drawdown halts are applied naturally (entries are blocked while the tracker is breached) rather than as a sticky "until Pranav resets" state.
- Prices in the backtest are split/bonus-adjusted for both signals and orders (raw prices would be needed for exact historical order prices).
- Open positions at the end stay in the equity curve but not in the trade list.
