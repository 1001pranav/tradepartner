# M2: Ledger, costs and tax, done

## What exists
- `costs/zerodha.py`: charges per order, allocated to fills exactly (largest remainder); STT rounding configurable; DP once per scrip-day on delivery sells. Rates come from `config/costs.yaml`.
- `ledger/lots.py`: pure FIFO matcher with same-day intraday netting, split/bonus handling, `unmatched` rows (never an invented cost basis). Order-independent and deterministic (property-tested).
- `ledger/tax.py`: categories (intraday_spec, stcg, ltcg, business, unmatched), strict `> 12 months` boundary, FY 31 Mar / 1 Apr boundary, STT deductible only for business-type income, loss set-off suggestions.
- `ledger/rebuild.py`: `tradepartner ledger rebuild` deletes and recreates `lots` and `realized`; idempotent.
- `ledger/importers.py`: Console tradebook CSV (unknown columns and non-NSE-equity fail loudly) and contract-note charges CSV (supersede computed charges).
- `ledger/sync.py`: Kite orders/trades/positions/holdings, origin from `tp-<id>` tags, order-event history, untagged-fill to proposal matching, position snapshots.
- `ledger/reconcile.py`: positions vs broker (qty exact, avg within 0.5%), realized vs Console summary (+/-Rs 10 per FY and category). Any mismatch writes a `block` risk event; `has_reconciliation_block()` is what the risk engine reads for AUTO.
- `ledger/corrections.py`: reversing + replacement rows, audit-logged.
- `reports/tax_export.py`: CSV + XLSX (summary, per-category sheets, charges, quarterly advance-tax view), all suggestions labelled "For CA review".
- UI: Ledger and P&L/Tax pages. CLI: `sync`, `ledger rebuild|import-tradebook|import-charges|reconcile`, `report tax`, `ui`.

## Acceptance
- Contract-note fixtures are within +/-Rs 1. **They are synthetic**, hand-computed by an independent script, because no real notes are in the repo. Add 3+ of your real contract notes to `tests/fixtures/contract_notes/` (same JSON shape) and confirm STT rounding [VERIFY].
- 100% branch coverage on `costs` and `ledger`.
- The "match Console Tax P&L within Rs 10 per FY" check needs your real tradebook: run `tradepartner ledger import-tradebook <csv>` and `tradepartner ledger reconcile --console-summary <csv>`.

## Spec questions (marked `# SPEC-QUESTION:` in code)
- Console tradebook has no product column, so rows are CNC and same-day round trips are charged at delivery rates (higher, so conservative) until contract notes are imported.
- Under `business_income` treatment only short-term delivery becomes `business`; long-term stays LTCG.
- Holdings are preferred over CNC day-positions in reconciliation to avoid double counting T+1 buys.
- Bonus lots open on the ex-date (allotment date is not in the data).
- `fills.source` gained a `correction` value for the correction flow.
